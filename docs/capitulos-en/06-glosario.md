# Glossary

- @Psyche — Marks on the sheet that measure mental wear from chrome and cyberpsychosis episodes. Thresholds: 1–3 brief; 4–5 major (scene); more than 5 = permanent cyberpsycho (GM's control). See *Cyberware*.

- Starting arsenal — The profession's starting weapon (1 street-grade weapon from its list). See *Creating a Cyberpunk*.

- Attributes — Four roll modifiers: NE, CM, KR, MT (see below).

- The Street — The city outside corporate privilege: the neighborhood, the black market, people without badges. Tone, not rules.

- Quality tiers — A piece's grade (chrome or hardware): Improvised (5 Credits), Street (10), Hi-Tech (15), Military (20) when buying new (includes 1 basic subsystem). See *Cyberware → Credits*.

- Hardware — Non-implanted gear (weapons, armor, tools, data in a slot, etc.). Adds no @Psyche. See *Hardware* and the catalog.

- Chrome / Cyberware — An implant integrated into the body. Counts toward implant load, humanity, and @Psyche (with exceptions, e.g. the Neuraslot). See *Cyberware* and the chrome catalog.

- Credit — The currency for buying chrome, hardware, accessories, and upgrades. 1 Star = 1 Credit.

- Cyberpsychosis — An episode (or state) of loss of control tied to chrome and @Psyche. See *Cyberware*.

- Neural degradation — For every 3 installed chrome pieces: −1 to an attribute of choice (min. −4) and 1 @Psyche box. See *Cyberware*.

- GM — Whoever runs the table: describes the situation, calls whether there's a roll, applies consequences (especially on 7–9 and 6−). Doesn't roll dice to "win" and doesn't compete against the players.

- NE (Neural Engine) — Attribute: thinking, remembering, hacking, running systems, using implants/tech.

- Star Scene — The session's most memorable moment, chosen by the players; can earn 1 Star if the character took part. See *Cyberware → Stars*.

- Star — The Virtual Appraiser's payment for Neurodata (and a creation resource: 5 when making the PC). Same currency as the Credit.

- Full success / partial success / miss — Roll results: 10+, 7–9, 6−. See *System*.

- Humanity — A fiction concept: how much human is left under the chrome. In the rules it's expressed mostly through @Psyche and neural degradation.

- ICE — Net slang: anti-intruder defenses. Color; combat on the Net resolves with the relevant table rules / chrome.

- CM (Cognitive Manipulation) — Attribute: reading people, persuasion, lying, social pressure, empathy.

- Move — A rule with a trigger in the fiction, a roll (if it applies), and results 10+ / 7–9 / (sometimes) 6−. If the character performs the trigger's action, the Move happens. Anyone can attempt it if the fiction allows; what changes are the tools (chrome/hardware).

- Neurodata — Lived experience downloaded to a terminal and sellable. The Appraiser pays for it in Stars. There's also neurodata as hardware (content in a Neuraslot): see the hardware catalog.

- Neuraslot — Chrome: slots for digital content. Doesn't count toward @Psyche. Number of slots per quality (1 / 4 / 8 / 16). Part of starting cyberware (1 improvised at character creation).

- Night City — The default stage-city for this manual's cyberpunk tone.

- PbtA — *Powered by the Apocalypse*: fiction first, rules second; you roll when there's risk, uncertainty, or serious consequences.

- Fumble — A critical failure at the table when using a chrome-boosted attribute: a 1-turn cyberpsychosis episode + 1 @Psyche box. See *Cyberware*.

- Profession — The creation archetype (just one). Fixes starting traits and arsenal. Background = fiction, not new rules.

- KR (Kinetic Response) — Attribute: reflexes, precision, dodging, acting under fire, timing.

- SAI — Smartgun system. Usually requires the smartgun link chrome piece. Recurring options: ionic accelerator, assisted aiming, ballistic flow, stabilizer. See the hardware catalog.

- Virtual Appraiser — Evaluates Neurodata and pays Stars per session criteria. See *Cyberware*.

- Roll — 2d6 + attribute when there's risk, uncertainty, or serious consequences. No roll if the outcome is clear and there's no interesting pressure. The players roll.

- MT (Muscular Tissue) — Attribute: strength, endurance, physical violence, enduring extreme conditions.

- Boost cap (+4) — The sum of chrome bonuses can't push any attribute above +4.

## Professions

| Profession | In one line |
| --- | --- |
