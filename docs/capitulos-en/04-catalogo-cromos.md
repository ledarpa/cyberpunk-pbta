# Chrome

## How to Read the Entries

- Every implant entry is a chrome piece (counts toward implant load and @Psyche, unless stated otherwise).

- Lines with > are modules or options that take up slots in the modular implant.

- Improvised / Street / Hi-Tech / Military = quality (new piece cost: 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 Credits).

> **Example — Sensory chrome in action**
> The Spook is on the ledge of the 41st floor, thin rain sliding down Arasaka's neon. She says: "I want to read the boardroom conversation without going in." The GM: "KR — your photosensor gives you +1." 2d6+KR: **9**.

>
> "I nudge the photosensor one degree and the half-light turns into gray noon. The ×10 optical zoom drops the boardroom onto my retina like I'm leaning on the table: three corpos, one briefcase. I switch to thermal and see the two guards behind the bulletproof glass, orange stains breathing. I open the laser mic, paint it against the window, and the cyberaudio brings me every word from 500 meters away like a whisper in my ear. Names, figures, a shipment. I record all of it. What I don't see is the hair-thin red line of the security laser crossing my ankle as I pull back. On some server, a quiet alert starts counting. I've got the data. Now I need to be gone before the count hits zero."

- Ink: opaque; disadvantage on the opponent's next KR roll; blinds organic or cybernetic optics until carefully removed.

## Cyberlimbs

Nerve, muscle, and bone adapters from the base of the shoulder (or equivalent).

- Multiple cyberarms or cyberlegs: one per amputated biological limb.

- Exception: additional-limb modules on a cyberspine → up to 4 extra limbs (per the implant).

- A limb replacing a biological one = one chrome piece; mounted only on a cyberspine → the spinal chrome counts, not an extra one per arm/leg.

### Cyberarm

#### Arachno-grapple

(Arm or extra arm.) Four hooks; climb and grab. Climbing without a roll; grapples and holds with advantage. +1 MT.

#### Combat arm

| **Magnetic shield** | Improv./Street.: personal shield, blocks 1d6 (Street. with advantage), KR roll vs the attack. Hi-T: full-body zone shield, you don't move, blocks without KR. Mil.: 3 m² zone, protects others/equipment; Hi-T/Mil.: low-power mode = personal shield. **+1 MT** |

> **Example — A cyberlimb in action**
> The Mercenary walks into the dock warehouse and the first guy is already pulling up the shotgun. "I drop him before he finishes aiming." The GM: "MT — go." 2d6+MT: **11**.
>
> "The combat arm pops open with a dry click and the Mantis Blade unfolds its meter of edge from the forearm. No time to think; the body is already inside. The first slash cuts the shotgun in two —1d6 with advantage, street-grade, and today the advantage weighs— and the second opens his vest from top to bottom. He drops without a sound. The partner comes in from the left shooting: I cross into the magnetic shield, the burst bounces off the field in blue sparks, the KR roll comes out clean and not one bullet touches me. One step, servo at full, and the Mantis finishes the conversation. Silence. Just the rain on the tin roof and the hum of the shield powering down. I count the bodies: two. I count my bullets: zero spent. The chrome did all the work. That's how you fight when the arm is worth more than the guy facing it."

#### Ballistic limb

Only on a cyberspine module. Integrated smartgun + automatic SAI link (see hardware). +1 KR.

> **Example — Ballistic limb**
> The guard sprints for the blast door console. "Don't let him seal it." The DJ: "KR, roll it." 2d6+KR: **9**.
>
> "The SAI paints the silhouette behind the glass before I even see it. The wrist turns, the barrel slides out and the shot fires itself: the guard drops behind the counter. Nobody's going to find the weapon: there isn't one."
